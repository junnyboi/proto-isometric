# GPT Image 2 Prompt Sheet: Biome Tiny Mobs

All four prompts share this production target: an original standalone game sprite on a true transparent background, 1:1 aspect ratio, full creature visible and centered, three-quarter isometric view facing down-right, soft upper-left key light, no floor plane, no cast shadow, no environment, no text, no border, no duplicate creatures, no detached parts. The rendering should match the existing Walker's Wake enemy art: painterly organic-mechanical creature design with crisp pixel-stepped edges, dark mechanical joints, weathered armor, restrained emissive accents, and simplified high-contrast material groups that remain readable at 42–48 gameplay pixels.

## Glassback Scarab

Create one tiny hostile **Glassback Scarab** game sprite for a desert biome. The creature is a low six-legged beetle with a broad oval body, overlapping ochre and rust-colored armor, a translucent amber-glass carapace, black iron joints, two shovel-like forked mandibles, a small hot-gold eye slit, and sand-abraded pale cream shell edges. Emphasize the broad back and forked front silhouette. Keep the body compact and the legs thick enough to survive downscaling. Avoid jewel-like decoration, realistic photographic insect detail, wings, scenery, dust clouds, and a ground shadow.

## Mire Tick

Create one tiny hostile **Mire Tick** game sprite for a wetland biome. The creature is a squat marsh parasite with a dark teal seed-pod abdomen, six thin but readable mud-caked legs, a circular lamprey-like mouth under the front plate, two short reed whiskers, small patches of moss, weathered bronze under-plates, and two dim cyan sensory nodes. Emphasize the round pod body, splayed needle-leg silhouette, and reed whiskers. Avoid a cute face, spider-web imagery, excessive slime, scenery, water surface, reeds in the background, and a ground shadow.

## Rime Shardling

Create one tiny hostile **Rime Shardling** game sprite for a frozen tundra biome. The creature is a compact four-legged ice arthropod with chipped white ceramic armor, a dark mechanical core, three large translucent blue crystal spines forming a triangular crown, hooked skating claws, a pointed armored front, and a narrow cyan eye slit. Frost dust rims the plates. Emphasize the three-spine crown and hooked four-leg silhouette. Avoid a mammal body, snow scenery, a large tail, magical particles, a ground plane, and a cast shadow.

## Ember Skitter

Create one tiny hostile **Ember Skitter** game sprite for a volcanic biome. The creature is a compact six-legged obsidian crawler with angular cracked basalt plates, vivid orange magma seams, two short furnace-horn vents, black hooked feet, a bright ember mouth, and a few cooled ash flakes clinging to the back. Emphasize the twin vents, angular shell, and incandescent crack pattern. Avoid fire surrounding the creature, smoke clouds, scenery, a lava floor, a ground shadow, and an overly large monster scale.
