# Walker’s Wake Peaceful Biome Alternates — Lyria 3 Pro Prompts

These prompts define four original instrumental alternates. They avoid named artists, works, recognizable melodies, vocals, synthetic-forward timbres, drum grooves, and abrupt intensity changes.

## Desert alternate — Night Sand, Open Sky

Instrumental only, no vocals. Create a 100-second original peaceful cinematic desert ambient cue at approximately 58 BPM in D Dorian. Make it acoustic, organic, spacious, contemplative, and live-feeling, as though a tiny ensemble is playing outdoors beneath a cold night sky. Use expressive solo cello, very soft viola harmonics, a breathy wooden flute with ney-like color, occasional muted oud-like plucked strings, and an almost inaudible low bowed drone. Ensure there are no drums, no hand percussion, no claps, no beat, no rhythmic ostinato, no trailer impacts, and no synthetic lead or obvious electronic pad. The arrangement must stay sparse, with long pauses and plenty of air between phrases. Weave quiet environmental recordings throughout: gentle desert wind moving across a wide stereo field, close sand grains shifting, soft dune slides, and occasional distant air gusts. Environmental sounds must remain smooth and non-startling, never masking gameplay cues. Write a fully original, restrained three-note wandering motif that feels curious rather than heroic. Keep dynamics between intensity 1/10 and 3/10. The opening and ending must share the same sustained D harmony, wind texture, instrumentation, and low intensity so the piece can form a seamless cyclic loop; do not use a final cadence or fade to silence.

[0:00 - 0:20] Begin with wind, shifting sand, one low cello harmonic, and a single answering flute breath. Intensity: 1/10.

[0:20 - 0:45] Introduce the original three-note motif on muted plucked strings, answered slowly by cello. Keep notes widely spaced. Intensity: 2/10.

[0:45 - 1:10] Let viola harmonics and wooden flute gently overlap while a distant dune slide passes through the stereo image. No pulse. Intensity: 3/10.

[1:10 - 1:30] Thin back to cello, flute breath, wind, and sparse sand movement. Return toward the opening harmony. Intensity: 2/10.

[1:30 - 1:40] Match the opening state exactly in mood and texture: sustained D, soft wind, one cello harmonic, no cadence, no fade-out. Intensity: 1/10.

## Wetlands alternate — Still Water Lanterns

Instrumental only, no vocals. Create a 100-second original peaceful wetlands ambient cue at approximately 54 BPM in F Lydian. Make it acoustic, intimate, warm, reflective, and gently alive, like a small chamber ensemble beside still water at dawn. Use alto flute, bass clarinet, soft legato cello, harp harmonics, very occasional muted wooden chime resonance, and natural reed breath. Ensure there are no drums, no beat, no groove, no rhythmic ostinato, no trailer percussion, no sharp attacks, and no synthetic lead or obvious electronic pad. The wooden chime may sound only once or twice as a distant tonal color, never as rhythm. Weave subtle environmental recordings through the cue: rounded water bubbles, tiny ripples, soft droplets, reeds moving in a light breeze, distant insects, and faint mud or water movement. Keep every environmental transient gentle and low in the mix so gameplay SFX remain clear. Compose a fully original rising four-note motif with long held resolutions and generous silence. Keep dynamics between intensity 1/10 and 3/10. The final seconds must return to the same open F harmony, water-bubble rhythmlessness, and alto-flute breath as the opening; no final cadence and no fade to silence.

[0:00 - 0:20] Open with quiet water bubbles, reeds, one harp harmonic, and a low bass-clarinet breath. Intensity: 1/10.

[0:20 - 0:45] State the four-note motif slowly on alto flute while cello holds warm open intervals. Intensity: 2/10.

[0:45 - 1:10] Add bass clarinet counter-breaths, sparse harp harmonics, two gentle droplets, and a wider reed soundscape. Intensity: 3/10.

[1:10 - 1:30] Remove the harp and let flute, cello, ripples, and distant insects settle. Intensity: 2/10.

[1:30 - 1:40] Recreate the opening’s open F harmony, bubble texture, and breathy instrumental spacing with no cadence or fade. Intensity: 1/10.

## Frozen Tundra alternate — Aurora Under Snow

Instrumental only, no vocals. Create a 100-second original peaceful frozen-tundra ambient cue at approximately 48 BPM in E minor with suspended seconds and open fifths. Make it acoustic, fragile, luminous, spacious, and calm rather than threatening. Use high violin and viola harmonics, a soft solo cello, bass flute, breathy oboe, and faint bowed-glass resonance that still feels physically performed. Ensure there are no drums, no percussion, no beat, no pulse, no rhythmic ostinato, no trailer impacts, no synthetic lead, and no obvious electronic pad. Weave environmental recordings through the full piece: wide howling wind softened by distance, powder snow sweeping across ice, low remote ice groans, and tiny crystalline cracks. Cracks must be delicate and infrequent, never sharp enough to resemble combat warnings. Compose a fully original five-note motif that rises slowly and dissolves into harmonics. Keep density extremely low and dynamics between intensity 1/10 and 3/10. The ending must return to the same E open fifth, wind direction, bowed-glass halo, and sparse harmonic texture as the opening; no conclusive cadence and no fade to silence.

[0:00 - 0:20] Begin with distant wind, one bowed-glass tone, an open cello fifth, and a barely audible snow sweep. Intensity: 1/10.

[0:20 - 0:45] Present the five-note motif on bass flute, with high string harmonics answering from far away. Intensity: 2/10.

[0:45 - 1:10] Add breathy oboe fragments, a low ice groan, and a slow harmonic shimmer without adding pulse or density. Intensity: 3/10.

[1:10 - 1:30] Let the oboe disappear and return focus to cello, wind, snow, and isolated harmonics. Intensity: 2/10.

[1:30 - 1:40] Match the opening E fifth, wind, glass halo, and near-silence; no cadence, no final swell, no fade-out. Intensity: 1/10.

## Lava Fields alternate — Embers After the Storm

Instrumental only, no vocals. Create a 100-second original peaceful volcanic ambient cue at approximately 50 BPM in C minor with suspended seconds and open fifths. Make it acoustic, dark-warm, meditative, spacious, and quietly resilient—the calm between eruptions, not a battle cue. Use low solo cello, contrabass harmonics, bass clarinet, contrabassoon breath, sparse bowed metal, and occasional soft stone resonance. Ensure there are no drums, no choir, no vocals, no beat, no rhythmic ostinato, no trailer hits, no aggressive brass, no synthetic lead, and no obvious electronic pad. Environmental recordings should be present but subdued: low lava bubbles, gentle ember hiss, distant muffled volcanic breaths, soft flame movement, and an occasional far rockfall with rounded transients. Do not use close explosions, fireballs, flamethrower blasts, or sharp cracks in this peaceful alternate. Compose a fully original descending four-note motif voiced slowly by cello and bass clarinet. Keep dynamics between intensity 1/10 and 3/10, with abundant silence. The final seconds must return to the same C open fifth, ember hiss, low lava bubbles, and contrabassoon breath as the opening; avoid a final cadence and do not fade to silence.

[0:00 - 0:20] Open with ember hiss, low lava bubbles, one C open fifth, and a breathy contrabassoon tone. Intensity: 1/10.

[0:20 - 0:45] Introduce the descending four-note motif on solo cello, answered by bass clarinet. Intensity: 2/10.

[0:45 - 1:10] Add contrabass harmonics, faint bowed metal, one distant volcanic breath, and a soft far rockfall. No pulse. Intensity: 3/10.

[1:10 - 1:30] Remove the metal resonance and leave cello, bass clarinet, embers, and lava bubbles. Intensity: 2/10.

[1:30 - 1:40] Recreate the opening C fifth, ember bed, bubbles, and contrabassoon breath without cadence or fade-out. Intensity: 1/10.
