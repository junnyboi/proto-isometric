## Sunscoured Sandstone Cluster

Subject: a low broad cluster of stacked desert sandstone slabs and rounded boulders, wind-eroded warm ochre rock, pale sun-bleached upper faces, restrained rust-red mineral seams, a few chipped corners and shallow cracks, substantial enough to block a walking mech. The silhouette should be asymmetric but compact, with two or three stepped height levels and a stable wide base.

Composition: three-quarter isometric view from above, complete object fully visible, centered on a square canvas with generous even padding, broad ground-contact footprint near the lower center, no cropping.

Style: polished hand-painted 2D game prop with crisp readable forms, compact realistic material detail, subtle painterly texture, clear light and shadow planes, consistent with the supplied frozen stone and volcanic obstacle references. Warm upper-left lighting, moderately saturated desert palette, no pixelation.

Constraints: true transparent background, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no scenery, no plants, no sand dune, no text, no UI, no border, no detached rubble or dust particles. Preserve a clean alpha edge and a single cohesive obstacle silhouette.

Avoid: cinematic environment art, floating rocks, thin fragile spires, caves, architecture, symbols, fossils, gemstones, tiny clutter, front-facing orthographic view, opaque checkerboard or colored background.
